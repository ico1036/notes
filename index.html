JW Lab

Working notes on research systems that run themselves. The one these are drawn from searches for stock-market trading strategies unsupervised — forming its own hypotheses, testing them against sixteen years of market data, and writing down why each one failed. Mostly about the parts that are not the model.

Rule 110, seeded at random on every load. The rule is fixed; the seed is not.
