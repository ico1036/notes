Technical Notes & Empirical Findings
  • 2026-08-10 · Track: for Agents & Quant · Note 03 Featured Paper
    Overfit and Dynamic

    A search loop with a fixed score will fit a moving market given enough attempts. The defence I borrowed is stigmergy — a trail that evaporates. It works, and it contains the flaw the analogy itself predicts: the maze never changes shape.

  • 2026-08-10 · Track: for Agents · Note 02
    Fat Skill, Thin Multi-Agents

    Two thousand lines of resident procedure against eighty-five lines of orchestration, and an ablation showing the procedure is worth +2.4 on a ten-point judgment task.

  • 2026-08-10 · Track: for Decision AI · Note 04
    Abstraction Layer, but Think First

    A search that records what failed learns nothing that transfers; it has to record why a kind of thing fails. Four tests that break a bad ontology — and what happens when you enforce them.

  • 2026-08-10 · Track: for Quant · Note 01
    Deterministic and Probabilistic

    Which of a system's decisions are allowed to be a sample from a distribution? Zero of fifty-eight pipeline files call a model. What that leaves is a verifiable failure boundary.

  • 2026-08-10 · Track: for Quant · Note 05
    Look-Ahead Bias and Path Dependency

    How do you test a program when you cannot state what its output should be? Asserting relations between runs rather than values from one: metamorphic testing against cheat programs.
